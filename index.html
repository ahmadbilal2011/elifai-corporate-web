Telecom and physical infrastructure

Fiber in the ground, light on the glass

We survey, install, splice, test and commission physical networks: fiber routes, DWDM transport, data center cabling and enterprise networks. Every link is tested before handover and documented so your operations team can trust the records.

Fiber optic installation

Underground, aerial and indoor fiber routes, from the first duct to the last splice, built to the route design and tested end to end.

  • Route surveys, wayleave support and civil works planning
  • HDPE duct and micro-duct, cable blowing and aerial ADSS
  • Fusion splicing, joint closures and ODF termination
  • FTTH and FTTx builds on GPON and XGS-PON

ITU-T G.652.D · G.657.A · ADSS · GPON · XGS-PON

DWDM and optical transport

DWDM ultra-high bandwidth technology: many wavelengths of light share one fiber pair, carrying traffic between cities, data centers and carrier points of presence.

  • Link budget, OSNR and dispersion design before anything is ordered
  • ROADM, multiplexer and amplifier installation and commissioning
  • Channel turn-up, bit-error-rate testing and alarms into your NOC
  • Capacity upgrades planned without service interruption

C-band · ROADM · EDFA · OTN · coherent optics

Structured cabling and data centers

Copper and fiber backbones for offices, campuses and data halls, installed neatly, labeled consistently and certified link by link.

  • Cat6A copper with OM4, OM5 and OS2 fiber backbones
  • Racks, containment, cable management and labeling to TIA-606
  • Every permanent link certified, with test reports in the handover pack
  • Meet-me rooms, cross-connects and data center moves

TIA-568 · TIA-606 · ISO/IEC 11801 · LC / MPO

Questions Gulf clients ask us first

If yours isn't here, message us on WhatsApp and an engineer will answer.

Can you host and operate our systems inside Saudi Arabia?

Yes. We build on Saudi cloud regions such as Google Cloud in Dammam, Oracle Cloud in Jeddah and Riyadh, and Alibaba Cloud in Riyadh, or in your own data center. Your data stays in the Kingdom and our engineers operate it over audited remote access.

Do you align with NCA and PDPL requirements?

We map the platforms we build and run to the National Cybersecurity Authority's Essential Cybersecurity Controls and Cloud Cybersecurity Controls, and to the Personal Data Protection Law, and we hand over the evidence your compliance team needs.

How does remote managed service work day to day?

You get named engineers, 24/7 monitoring, a P1 response target of 15 minutes, monthly patching and backup checks, and a monthly service review. Access is through named accounts with multi-factor login, and every change goes through your approval process.

Which platforms do you support?

Red Hat Enterprise Linux, Ubuntu, Red Hat OpenShift, Kubernetes on AWS, Google Cloud, Oracle Cloud and Alibaba Cloud, VMware private cloud, and on-premise equipment from vendors such as Dell, HPE, Cisco, Fortinet and Palo Alto Networks.

Do you install fiber, DWDM and network cabling?

Yes. We survey, install, splice and test fiber routes, commission DWDM optical transport, and install certified structured cabling and enterprise networks. In Pakistan our own field teams work on site; across the Gulf we deliver with licensed local installation partners, with our engineers leading design, testing and acceptance.

How quickly can you start?

A typical onboarding takes two to four weeks: access and paperwork in week one, monitoring and runbooks in weeks two and three, then a handover review before we take the pager.

Do you only work in Saudi Arabia?

Saudi Arabia and the Gulf are our focus, and we also serve clients in Pakistan, Europe, the UK and Asia-Pacific remotely, with working hours that overlap Riyadh, Dubai and Central European time.
