Privacy notice

Last updated 8 November 2026

This notice explains what happens to the information you send through elifai.pk. The site is operated by Sysnetrix Pvt Ltd, headquartered in Islamabad, Pakistan, which runs Elifai. We keep it short because we collect very little.

What we collect

  • The details you type into the enquiry form: your name, work email, company, phone number, region, topic and message.
  • The IP address and time of the submission, which we use only to block spam and abuse.
  • Standard web-server logs kept by our hosting provider, such as the pages requested and the browser type.

What we do not collect

The site sets no advertising or analytics cookies and runs no third-party tracking scripts. Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, which receives your IP address when it serves the font files.

How we use it

We use enquiry details only to reply to you and to prepare any proposal you ask for. We do not sell them, rent them or add you to a mailing list without asking.

Where it is stored and for how long

Enquiries are delivered by email to info@elifai.pk and kept for up to 24 months after our last contact with you, unless they become part of a contract, in which case contractual retention periods apply.

Your rights

You can ask us to show, correct or delete the information we hold about you at any time. Where they apply to you, we handle requests in line with Pakistan's data-protection requirements, the Saudi Personal Data Protection Law and the EU and UK GDPR.
